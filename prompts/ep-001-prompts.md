# Image Prompts — Episode One: *The Small Yeses*

Six images. Save into `images/ep-001/` with **exactly** these filenames; the page already points at them.

**Attach on every prompt below:** everything in `images/_reference/`, leading with the two portraits.

**Two rules that bite when forgotten:**
- Any frame with both principals states the size ratio: **Thomas is ~1.4× Carolyn's height and ~2× her
  width; her head reaches his white neck ring.**
- Any apology adds to the negative block: **tears, crying, wet eyes, trembling mouth, downcast eyes, looking
  away, pleading.** The model draws "sorry" as "crying." An apology is level, not distressed.

## Blocks used in every prompt

**[STYLE LOCK]** — paste unchanged

## 01-hero.png — 16:9

**[SCENE]** The Kitchen Royal, late morning. Carolyn stands at a long wooden counter building a sofrito — a
heap of green peppers, a wooden board, a knife mid-work — sleeves pushed to the elbow, locs tied back with a
green cloth. Pim balances on a step stool at her elbow gripping a wooden spoon in both paws like a fishing
rod. Behind them Thomas entirely fills the main doorway. Copper pots, hanging garlic, a rack of wooden spoons
on the far wall, low sun through an open shutter.

**[COMPOSITION]** Wide establishing interior, 16:9, mid-distance, headroom at top. **Thomas is enormous —
roughly 1.4× Carolyn's height and twice her width — and the doorway barely contains him.**

**[MOOD]** Warm, busy, funny. A kitchen people actually live in.

## 02-beat.png — 4:3 *(the snag)*

**[SCENE]** Marketrow at midday. Auntie Yolanda has come out from behind her spice stall with both wings
spread wide toward Carolyn, blocking her path while appearing to greet her. Baskets of dried chiles,
allspice, bundled herbs behind her. Carolyn holds a market basket and is laughing, already half-committed.
Stalls and townsfolk — mostly pigs — blurred behind.

**[COMPOSITION]** Medium two-shot, 4:3, slightly low angle so Yolanda's spread wings dominate.

**[MOOD]** Comic ambush. Entirely affectionate.

## 03-beat.png — 4:3 *(the wobble)*

**[SCENE]** The Kitchen Royal, empty and still. Pim alone on the step stool, reaching up on tiptoe to hang a
wooden spoon back on its hook, handle turned outward. Her too-large apron still on. The pot on the stove is
cold. Afternoon light in a long bar across the empty floor. Nobody else in the room.

**[COMPOSITION]** Wider than feels comfortable, 4:3, Pim small and off-center, a great deal of empty kitchen
around her.

**[MOOD]** Quiet. Not tragic — just far too quiet for a kitchen. Restraint carries this one.

## 04-beat.png — 4:3 *(the hinge)*

**[SCENE]** The Kitchen Royal at dusk. A covered soup pot sits heavily on the table between Carolyn and
Thomas, mid-argument — she is upright and level, he has drawn himself up to full towering height with one
wing raised, wing tip splayed into a point. In the lit doorway behind them stands Fig Bramblewick, a tiny
piglet with a basket of eggs on her hip, looking straight up at Thomas and asking a question. Neither adult
has noticed her.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3, the two adults left and center, Fig small and clear in the doorway at right. The eye
should find her second. **Thomas towers; Carolyn's head reaches his neck ring.**

**[MOOD]** Tense but not frightening. The piglet is about to end the argument.

## 05-repair.png — 4:3 *(the repair — the most important image in the episode)*

**[SCENE]** The Kitchen Royal, evening. Carolyn is **sitting down on the kitchen floor**, skirts gathered,
so that she is at eye level with Pim, who stands beside a scrubbing board with a cloth still in her paws.
Carolyn's chin is level and her gaze is steady and directed straight at Pim — calm, warm, undefensive, saying
something true. Pim is listening. On the wall behind them, the rack of wooden spoons. **Two small yellow
butterflies have come in over the sill; one has settled on the spoon rack and stayed there.** The butterflies
are quiet and incidental — no glow, no swirl, no magical effect.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3, low camera at floor height so the viewer sits where Carolyn sits. Both faces clearly
readable. The butterfly on the rack is small and easy to miss on a first look.

**[MOOD]** Still, warm, and steady. This is not a sad picture.

**[NEGATIVE — add for this image]** tears, crying, wet eyes, trembling mouth, downcast eyes, looking away,
pleading, distressed, anguished, glowing butterflies, magical sparkles, swirling light.

---

## 06-close.png — 4:3 *(the warm close)*

**[SCENE]** The Long Table in the courtyard at dusk, laid end to end — a great pot of rice, stacked loaves,
cones of fried plantain, bowls of pepper sauce. The whole kingdom along both sides, **mostly pigs of every
size and age**, with Carolyn and Pim together near the middle and Thomas at one end taking up the space of
three. Lanterns strung overhead. A few yellow butterflies drifting through the lantern light.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3, table receding at a slight angle for depth, warm pools of lantern light, faces
readable.

**[MOOD]** The best hour of the week. Golden, full, noisy.
