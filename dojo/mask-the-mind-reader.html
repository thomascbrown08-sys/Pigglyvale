    The Mind-Reader

    “I know how you really feel about me.”

    “I know how you really feel about me.”

    Train it if it is worn at you you feel you have to prove what you were really thinking — if being told your own motive sends you hunting for evidence, and you can still remember arguments you won on the facts and lost anyway.

    Train it if you reach for it you say why somebody is being off with you as though it were a fact you had checked. This one is built out of paying attention, which is why it fits people who are good at it.

    Bruno wears this one and is enjoying it far too much. Pick a belt and go in.

    Not offered. Being told what you are thinking is never quite nothing-aimed; it is at minimum a thing said about you rather than to you.
