## Blocks used in every prompt

### [STYLE LOCK] — paste unchanged

> **[STYLE LOCK]** Warm storybook illustration, gouache and soft ink linework, hand-painted texture, visible
> brush edges. Sun-warmed palette: deep plum, mango gold, guava pink, herb green, buttermilk paper.
> Rounded, friendly character shapes. Soft directional light, late-afternoon. Children's picture-book
> sensibility with adult compositional care. Flat-ish depth, no heavy rendering.

### [NEGATIVE] — paste unchanged

> **[NEGATIVE]** photorealism, 3D render, CGI, anime or manga styling, harsh shadows, dark or grim
> atmosphere, horror, text or lettering in the image, watermarks, extra limbs, uncanny human faces.

*Why those two are built the way they are: Quill is the only character on the site whose silhouette is
vertical and closed, which is the whole point of her. Marisol is deliberately the anti-Carolyn — tall where
Carolyn is short, lean where she is round, indigo and oatmeal where she is plum and mango, silver-cropped
where she is loc'd and gold-banded, and twenty years older. Nothing in the palette should rhyme.*

## 01 · `01-hero.png` — 16:9 — Marisol arrives

*Attach: `marisol-sheet.png`, `carolyn-portrait.png` (for style).*

**[SCENE]** The top of a small market street on a bright morning. A travelling baker has just set down the
handles of a two-wheeled handcart carrying a barrel-shaped domed clay oven, sooted and still faintly
smoking. She stands beside it with her hands on her hips, mid-greeting, entirely unbothered. A crowd of
townsfolk — **mostly pigs**, a goose and an otter among them — is drifting toward her from the stalls, and
several are already leaning in toward the oven. Striped awnings, open baskets of dried chiles, a wall of
warm colour.

**[COMPOSITION]** Wide, 16:9. The baker and her oven off-centre left, the crowd curving in from the right so
the eye travels along the street. Full figures, low sun coming down the road behind her.

**[MOOD]** An arrival. Bright, busy, and genuinely welcome — nothing ominous, nothing being foreshadowed.
The kingdom is pleased and it is right to be.

## 02 · `02-beat.png` — 4:3 — the register, being kept

*Attach: `quill-sheet.png`, `carolyn-portrait.png`, `marisol-portrait.png`.*

**[SCENE]** The Kitchen Royal: whitewashed walls, a long scrubbed worktable, copper pots on a rail, garlic
and herbs hung to dry, a rack of wooden spoons, shuttered windows onto green. In the foreground the small
hedgehog stands with a large ledger open flat on the table, pencil in hand, waiting. The pig princess is
beside her, signing a page, mid-conversation, warm and unbothered. Behind them at the far end of the table
the tall silver-haired baker is working a piece of dough, sleeves rolled, not part of the conversation.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3. Three figures across the table in a shallow row. The ledger is the brightest object
in the frame. The hedgehog is much smaller than the princess and stands very straight.

**[MOOD]** Ordinary administration on a good day. Comic in its smallness. Nobody is worried about anything.

## 03 · `03-beat.png` — 4:3 — Marketrow, and the wrong listener

*Attach: `marisol-sheet.png`, `quill-portrait.png`.*

**[SCENE]** Midday at the market. The tall silver-haired baker stands among barrels of spice with a ring of
townsfolk around her — **mostly pigs**, six or seven of them, all laughing. She is mid-story, one arm out,
delighted. In the middle distance, on the far side of the barrels and slightly separated from the group,
the small hedgehog has stopped walking. She holds a shopping basket on one arm. She is looking straight
ahead, not at the group, and she is completely still.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3. The laughing group fills the left two-thirds in warm light. The hedgehog stands
alone in the right third, smaller in the frame, with a little empty space around her that nobody else has.
Same daylight on both — she is not in shadow and not spotlit.

**[MOOD]** A joke landing on one person differently from everybody else. No menace, no glare, no villain
staging. Just a person hearing something, in a bright market, in the middle of the day.

**[ADD TO NEGATIVE]** glaring, scowling, sinister expression, dramatic shadow on the observing figure,
tears, crying

## 04 · `04-beat.png` — 4:3 — the records room

*Attach: `quill-key-beats.png`, `carolyn-key-beats.png`.*

**[SCENE]** A small records room, late afternoon. Shelves of bound ledgers, a high window standing open,
dust in the light. The small hedgehog sits behind a desk with a big open ledger in front of her and her
pencil set down and squared exactly to the edge of the page. Her chin is level. The pig princess stands on
the other side of the desk with the green work cloth in her locs, one hand on the desk edge, having just
stopped talking — mouth closed, weight back, the anger already going out of her face.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3, from just above desk height so the seated hedgehog is not made small by the angle.
The desk runs across the frame between them. Real air between the two figures.

**[MOOD]** The second after a sharp thing has been said and heard. Tense, quiet, and entirely
non-threatening. Both of them are people, neither is a problem.

**[ADD TO NEGATIVE]** tears, crying, wet eyes, trembling mouth, shouting, snarling, pointing finger,
cowering

## 05 · `05-repair.png` — 4:3 — **the repair. Mandatory.**

*Attach: `carolyn-apology.png`, `quill-key-beats.png`.*

**[SCENE]** The same records room, a few minutes later, warmer light. Both are now **seated**, facing each
other across the corner of the desk. The pig princess sits with her hands in her lap and is not reaching
for anything; she is looking directly at the hedgehog, chin level, mouth closed, brows soft and open,
entirely undefensive. The hedgehog sits on the front third of her chair, spines flat, both hands flat on
her knees, listening — receiving it, not yet answering. On the shelf behind them, three yellow butterflies;
one has settled on the spine of an old ledger.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3. Both figures in the frame at the same eye level, close but not crowded. The
butterflies are small and unremarked, in the background, not haloed or glowing.

**[MOOD]** Level and steady and quiet. This is not a sad picture. An apology is not falling apart; it is
looking someone in the eye and saying the true thing, and that is a *calmer* face than distress, not a
sadder one.

**[ADD TO NEGATIVE — non-negotiable for this slot]** tears, crying, weeping, wet eyes, trembling mouth,
open mouth, downcast eyes, looking away, pleading, distressed, anguished, kneeling, clasped hands, halo,
glow, sparkles, magical effects around the butterflies

## 06 · `06-close.png` — 4:3 — the Long Table, and two people leaving it

*Attach: `scale-pair.png` (for the ratio), `carolyn-portrait.png`, `quill-portrait.png`,
`thomas-portrait.png`.*

**[SCENE]** A stone courtyard at dusk, paper lanterns strung overhead in warm gold and plum. A long table
runs down the frame with benches both sides, crowded with townsfolk — **overwhelmingly pigs** — eating.
Split loaves of soft bread, a big pot of rice with pigeon peas, a dish of greens. At the near end sits an
enormous mallard drake at his own separate small table, because he does not fit on a bench. Away up the
lane beyond the courtyard, small and seen from behind, a short round pig woman and a very small hedgehog
walk off into the dark together, the pig woman carrying a covered plate.

**[COMPOSITION]** 4:3. The lit table fills the lower two-thirds; the lane and the two departing figures sit
small in the upper right, well beyond the lantern light. **The drake is 1.4× the height and twice the width
of any pig at the table, and his head reaches well above the seated crowd — if he looks the same size as
the diners, the image is wrong.**

**[MOOD]** A warm, full, noisy table, and two people quietly leaving it to go and do something. Ending on
an errand, not on a speech.

## `quill-key-beats.png` — 16:9 — **the one that matters**

*Attach `quill-portrait.png`.*

**[SCENE]** An expression reference strip in the identical style to the attached image. The same character's
head and shoulders repeated three times against a flat buttermilk background, showing:
**(1)** correct and closed — reading out a rule she knows is right, spines slightly up, chin level, no
malice in it at all;
**(2)** apologizing, badly and sincerely — a face that has not done this before, chin level, eyes lifted and
looking directly at the other person, mouth closed;
**(3)** **the moment of being seen** — hearing something true about herself said kindly, spines gone
completely flat, eyes steady and open, entirely undefended, not smiling and not crying.

**[COMPOSITION]** Wide, 16:9, three evenly spaced heads at the same scale, single row.

**[ADD TO NEGATIVE — this image only]** tears, crying, weeping, wet eyes, trembling mouth, sour expression,
sneering, pursed disapproving mouth, glaring, downcast eyes, looking away, pleading, anguished

*Face three is the butterfly moment and there is no picture of what that looks like on a hedgehog. The model
will offer two wrong answers and both are wrong in the same way: a crying woman or a sour one. What is
actually happening is that a wall came down — so the spines go flat and the eyes stay up. Roll it until you
get that.*
