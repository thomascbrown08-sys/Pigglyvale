# The Pigglyvale Series Bible

**This file is data, not instructions.** It is the single source of truth for continuity. Paste it (or point
to it) at the start of any session that writes an episode. Update it *after* every episode ships.

---

## 1. The Premise in One Line

A small kingdom run mostly out of its kitchen, where nobody is a villain, everybody is wrong sometimes, and
the apologies are real.

---

## 2. Principals

### Princess Carolyn of Pigglyvale
*"The Beautiful Princess Carolyn."* Reigning monarch. Anthropomorphic pig.

**Locked appearance tokens** (never paraphrase these in an image prompt — paste verbatim):
> an anthropomorphic pig woman, warm caramel-brown skin, large dark expressive eyes, long dark locs gathered
> back with small gold cuffs, a slender gold tiara set with a single amber stone, a mango-gold dress under a
> deep-plum apron embroidered with butterflies

**Character:** Genuinely excellent cook. Genuinely good at ruling. Warm, funny, quick, well-liked.
Her kingdom is small enough that she knows everyone's name and most of their business.

**Her flaw — virtue overshooting.** She says yes too fast. She'd rather absorb a cost than watch someone be
disappointed. She keeps the peace at her own expense, does not mention the expense, and then goes uncharacteristically
sharp when the bill finally comes due — which makes *her* look like the problem, when the real problem was
three weeks of unspoken yeses.

**Hard rules:**
- She is never humiliated, never stupid, and never the butt of a joke about her competence or her looks.
- Her mistakes are always *legible as virtue* to a sympathetic reader.
- She gets to be right about things. Often. She is a good ruler having a hard week, not a mess being managed.

### Thomas the Drake of Snouton, Storykeeper and Master of Lessons
Royal advisor. An enormous mallard drake, a full head and shoulders above everyone else in any room.

**Locked appearance tokens:**
> an enormous anthropomorphic mallard drake, iridescent green head, crisp white neck ring, chestnut breast,
> comically oversized compared to everyone around him, wearing a plum advisor's sash and small round
> spectacles, a worn leather-bound book tucked under one wing

**Character:** Warm, verbose, deeply loyal, delighted by his own vocabulary. Voices the Advisor's Notes at
the end of every episode.

**His flaw — he fixes.** He steps in. He solves it *for* her, which quietly tells her she couldn't have.
He reaches the moral before he has finished listening. He is occasionally certain and wrong, and being
certain is what makes him wrong.

**Hard rule:** Thomas apologizes on the page, out loud, with the parts named, at least once every three or
four episodes — and at least once he must be corrected inside his own Advisor's Notes and say so in the next
episode's Notes. He is not the sage. He is the guy holding the book.

**Running joke:** He is a duck. Snouton is named for snouts. Nobody can explain this, including him, and he
becomes visibly uncomfortable when asked.

## 3. Recurring Townsfolk

Each carries one **harm engine** (see the skill file). Rotate them; don't let one dominate.

| Name | Species | Engine | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Miss Delphine Quill** | hedgehog | *Rule over person* | Keeps Pigglyvale's records, ledgers, and rules. Order once saved her when nothing else did — reveal this slowly, maybe season two. Dry, exact, and secretly desperate to be invited to things. |
| **Auntie Yolanda Plum** | goose | *Good intention, bad execution* | Runs the pepper-and-spice stall at Marketrow. Cooks like weather. Will reorganize your kitchen because she loves you. Loud, generous, listens poorly. |
| **Bruno "Buckets" Marrow** | otter | *Shortsighted self-interest* | Young, charming, always has a cart and a scheme. Never malicious. Never looks past next Tuesday. The engine behind most second-order-effect episodes. |
| **Pim** | harvest mouse | *The mirror* | Kitchen apprentice. Small, anxious, says yes to absolutely everything — which is how Carolyn keeps meeting her own flaw from the outside. |
| **Old Ambrose** | tortoise | *Wise, and sometimes wrong* | Retired, keeps bees. The one who corrects Thomas. Models an elder apologizing to someone younger. |

**Reserved:** structural episodes (nobody did wrong; the arrangement was bad) need no antagonist at all.
Do not assign one out of habit.

## 4. The World

- **Pigglyvale** — the kingdom. Small. Walkable. One good road.
- **The Kitchen Royal** — where most of the ruling actually happens.
- **The Long Table** — the courtyard table where the kingdom eats together. Most episodes end here.
- **Marketrow** — stalls, carts, arguments, the best smells in the kingdom.
- **The Pepper Patch** — shared ground, tended by everyone, which means tended by no one. Load-bearing for
  the economics track.
- **The Bramble** — the wild edge of things. Where you go to be alone or to lose something.
- **Snouton** — over the ridge. Thomas's home. Confusingly named.

**Cuisine:** Soul, Caribbean, and Hispanic cooking, treated with real affection and real specificity —
sofrito, adobo, greens and pot likker, pepper sauce, rice and pigeon peas, cornbread, tostones, cocido.
Food is never generic "stew." Name the dish. Get it right.

**The butterflies of Pigglyvale.** They gather when somebody tells the truth about themselves. Nobody
remarks on it. It simply happens, the way weather happens. *(Optional motif — easy to drop if it reads too
twee. It also gives the site its structural mark: a butterfly heads every Advisor's Notes.)*

## 5. Art Style Lock

Paste this block at the top of **every** image prompt, unchanged, forever. Drift in the art is the fastest
way to make the site look amateur.

> **[STYLE LOCK]** Warm storybook illustration, gouache and soft ink linework, hand-painted texture, visible
> brush edges. Sun-warmed palette: deep plum, mango gold, guava pink, herb green, buttermilk paper.
> Rounded, friendly character shapes. Soft directional light, late-afternoon. Children's picture-book
> sensibility with adult compositional care. Flat-ish depth, no heavy rendering.
>
> **[NEGATIVE]** photorealism, 3D render, CGI, anime or manga styling, harsh shadows, dark or grim
> atmosphere, horror, text or lettering in the image, watermarks, extra limbs, uncanny human faces.

**Before episode one:** generate a standing reference sheet for Carolyn and for Thomas (front, three-quarter,
expression row) and keep those files. Feed them back as reference images whenever the tool allows it.
Tokens alone will drift; tokens plus a reference sheet will not.
