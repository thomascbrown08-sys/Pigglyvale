# Episode 003 — *Quantity Not Stated* · image prompts

**Six slots. Paste ONE slot at a time.** Never paste this file whole — every token block is inlined in
every slot on purpose, and pasting the lot puts every character in one frame (pipeline rule 14).

**The tool has one text box and no negative field.** Everything named gets drawn, so every exclusion below
is written as a positive fact — *her eyes are dry and her mouth is closed*, never *no tears*. To keep a
character out of a frame, they are not named and their sheet is not attached (rules 13, 16, 17).

**Attach the sheets listed under each slot.** Text tokens hold costume and colour; the sheets hold face and
proportion. Neither works alone.

---

## The style paragraph — paste at the head of every slot, unchanged

> Warm storybook illustration, gouache and soft ink linework, hand-painted texture with visible brush edges.
> A sun-warmed palette of deep plum, mango gold, guava pink, herb green and buttermilk paper. Rounded,
> friendly character shapes. Soft directional light, late afternoon. Children's picture-book sensibility with
> adult compositional care, flat-ish depth, light rendering. Every face in the picture is an animal face with
> a snout, a bill or a muzzle. The picture is entirely wordless: every surface, page, label and sign is blank
> and unmarked. The atmosphere is warm, bright and domestic throughout.

## Notes for whoever rolls these

- **No butterflies in any slot this episode.** Deliberate. They were spent in Episode 002 and are being held.
- **Slot 03 names no individual pigs**, because Gus Thornapple has no approved appearance tokens and no
  reference image. Naming him would draw a guess. He is in the scene in the prose and out of it in the art
  until he has a token block — same gap as Bruno, and it is now the last one before Episode 004.
- **Slots 01, 02 and 05 all place two characters together**, so each states the relative height in its own
  composition block. Stating it once at the top of a file has never held (pipeline rule 8).
- **Slot 06 is the only frame with Thomas**, and it carries both the scale sentence and the single-sash
  sentence (rule 19), because he is seated in a busy frame, which is exactly when the sash doubles.
