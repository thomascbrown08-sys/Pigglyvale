    The Helper

    “I was protecting you.”

    “I was protecting you.”

    Train it if it is worn at you you cannot refuse a kindness without feeling ungrateful — if a favour you never asked for leaves you smaller, and saying so feels impossible because they meant well.

    Train it if you reach for it you step in before you ask. If watching somebody you love struggle is unbearable to you, and the asking gets skipped because asking risks an answer you do not want.

    Thomas wears this one, on the grounds that it is his and he ought to know it best. Pick a belt and go in.
