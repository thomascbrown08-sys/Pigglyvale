The Chronicles of Pigglyvale

Stories from a small kingdom run mostly out of its kitchen

Her Majesty the Beautiful Princess Carolyn rules a kingdom of about two hundred souls, one good road, and a pepper patch nobody can agree about. This is the record of what happens there.

Set down faithfully, and with only a little embellishment, by Thomas the Drake of Snouton, Storykeeper and Master of Lessons.

The Episodes

Newest at the top. Every episode ends with the Advisor's Notes, in which Thomas explains what he thinks happened — and, on occasion, admits he had it backwards.

About this place

Pigglyvale is small on purpose. Nobody here is a villain, everybody here is wrong sometimes, and the apologies are real ones — said out loud, with the parts named. That is most of what the kingdom is for.

Made for Carolyn.

The line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being.
